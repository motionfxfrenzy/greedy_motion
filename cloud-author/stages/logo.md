# Static logo animation

Use the supplied logo pixels or vectors unchanged. The Wiggle and Lottie references inform motion metaphor, element separation, looping and file validation. Keep the native logo and wordmark legible, respect brand colors and avoid inventing a replacement mark. For an embedded film, synchronize the resulting vector asset or DOM animation to HyperFrames. Deliver a preview and ensure the logo lands clearly at the last frame.

A mark arrives with the weight of a panel (controlled settle through the `spring-settle` recipe, damping 0.75-0.9), not a mascot bounce, unless the brand's motion profile is springy. Measure the supplied mark's real bounding box before placing or scaling it, never redraw it, and make the final lockup the poster frame: still readable, one focal point, nothing mid-transition.

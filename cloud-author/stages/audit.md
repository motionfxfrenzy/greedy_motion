# Motion review

Inspect representative first, middle, transition and final frames, and seek backward through the composition. Check text size and contrast, asset loading, clipping, overlaps, brand fidelity, pace and unwanted stillness. Run HyperFrames lint/render and project gates. Diagnose issues in source; do not suppress a warning simply to pass an audit.

Audit as a critic, not an author: judge what the source will draw, not what it intends. Score hook (something moves or reads in the first 0.5 s), readability at phone size (effective text size is font-size times every ancestor scale; floors 5.2% of the shorter edge for captions, 3.0% for other read text), continuity (every handoff carries a shared element), motion (one weight per object class, no idle wobble, no overshoot on opacity, camera never in-then-out or reversing a pan), sound sync (cues on the frame of their event). Report at most the three largest defects, each with a timestamp or element, the evidence quoted from the source, and a minimal local fix; propose edits only for those, and say what could not be judged from source alone (it needs a rendered frame). Look specifically for the source patterns behind single-frame pops and seek failures: a bare `tl.set` mid-timeline, a state flip split across two frames, an animated property missing from `from` or `to`, a missing identity baseline, a layer without a z-index, `Math.random` or a clock, a CSS transition.

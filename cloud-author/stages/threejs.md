# Three.js scene

Use this stage only when the shot needs real 3D geometry, lighting or camera movement. Read the relevant Three.js skill chapters for geometry, materials, lighting, animation, loaders, shaders or postprocessing. Tie camera, mixer and shader time to the HyperFrames playhead and test arbitrary/reverse seeks. Verify local assets, aspect framing, render cost and a deterministic fallback when WebGL is unavailable. Do not create 3D solely because a style sounds dimensional; a generated material clip or 2D design may be more faithful.

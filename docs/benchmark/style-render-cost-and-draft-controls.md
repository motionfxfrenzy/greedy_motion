## 1. Architectural Principles: Why Rendering Cost Is Style-Specific & Why `draftTargetVerticesPerFrame` Is Not a Hard Polygon Cap

1. **Every Native Style Has a Distinct Physical Medium & Cost Driver**:
   - `@videosaas/plate-engine` contains **31 native 2D canvas styles** (`packages/plate-engine/manifest.json`). They do **not** share a single universal bottleneck:
     - **`Gfx.pen` + `hatchRuns` styles** (`balloon`, `pocketWatch`, `moonPhases`, `blueprint`, `koi`, `ranunculus`, `rubberHose`, `scrapbook`, `storybook`, `wren`) spend CPU and raster time expanding Catmull-Rom splines into variable-width polygons (`ribbon()`) and probing scanline hatching (`hatchRuns()`).
     - **Point-field / screen-dot styles** (`stipple`, `halftone`) spend **cold cache build** time computing continuous-tone separation rasters (`shellTone`, `buildTone`) and generating `26,380`–`66,909` dots (`Path2D` chunks or plate layers), while **warm advancing frames** composite cached checkpoint/sheet layers in `1.5–2.8 ms`.
     - **Single-spiral engraving (`mellan`)** caches its spiral walk (`ribbon()`), but on **every warm advancing frame** calls `ctx.fill()` on one single self-winding polygon with `156,600`–`429,038` vertices (`500` active scanline edge intersections per row at `PITCH = 6.2 px`).
     - **Multi-ply bristle / stitch / cut-mark kits** (`paintedOil`, `brokenColour`, `sumiE`, `embroidery`, `colouredPencil`, `scratchboard`, `folkTale`) spend time generating sub-strokes (bristle hairs, thread twists, pencil grain marks, burin cuts) and compiling progressive pass checkpoints.
     - **Per-pixel `getImageData` / `putImageData` drum/tile styles** (`lighthouse` via `riso.ts`, `woodcut` via `washi`, `charcoalErasure`, `flatVector`) spend CPU time in per-pixel loops (cached once for static tiles, or run per-frame in `riso.ts`).
     - **Low-vertex geometric / grid styles** (`flatVector` `708` verts / `0.3 ms`, `pixelArt` `9,656` verts / `0.8 ms`, `isometric` `1,527` verts / `2.5 ms`, `lowPoly` `26,976` verts / `8.3 ms`) are already well under `10 ms` per frame in Full mode.
2. **Why `draftTargetVerticesPerFrame` Is a Soft Scene-Calibrated Target, Not a Hard Polygon Cap**:
   - Enforcing a hard vertex cap by dropping strokes or clipping polygons after a counter reaches `B` would erase semantic objects (eyes, text, hands, arches) or cause temporal flicker as strokes enter/leave the cap across frames.
   - Instead, `draftTargetVerticesPerFrame` (`75,000` default) drives **deterministic, spatially stable density controls** (`effectiveScaleRatio`, `materialDetailScale`, Poisson dot spacing `spScale`, AM screen pitch `effPitch`, bristle `hairStride`, stitch-twist `step`, spiral `arcStep`) subject to **protected minimum floors** (`minClosed`, `MIN_PROTECTED_STIPPLE_DOTS`, `MIN_PROTECTED_HALFTONE_DOTS`, and `mellan`'s 125-turn spiral structure), setting `budgetExceededByFloor = true` whenever the protected floor exceeds `targetB`.
   - **Design Intent vs Pixel Proof**: Density-compensation formulas (such as scaling dot radius `radScale`, bristle `lineWidth`, or burin width `w = effPitch * ...`) are **design intents** to preserve optical tone and outer subject bounds, **not mathematical proofs of pixel equivalence**. Where material marks define the image (e.g., `mellan` spiral pitch, `stipple` dots, `halftone` rosettes), changing mark spacing alters pixel-level foreground coverage and PSNR and requires **explicit owner visual review**.
3. **Do Not Conflate Geometry Vertices (`totalSceneVertices`) with Material Operations (`kitMaterialOpsReduced`)**:
   - `totalSceneVertices` counts polygon/path vertices submitted to `CanvasRenderingContext2D` / `Path2D`, whereas `kitMaterialOpsReduced` counts kit-level material reductions (such as 2×2 pixel sub-sampling in `getImageData` tiles, skipped bristle/twist passes, or reduced fountain-key strips).

## 2. Immediate `mellan` (`illus-mellan`) Experiment: Spiral Turn-Pitch (`effPitch`) vs Curve-Sampling (`arcStep`) & Sub-4k (`2k / 1k / 500`) Sweep

### 2.1 Why Spiral Turn-Pitch (`effPitch`) Outperforms `arcStep` Alone at Equal Vertex Count (and Its Visual Tradeoff)
- In `packages/plate-engine/src/canvas-core/mellan.ts`, `walk(maxStep, arcStep, effPitch)` walks an Archimedean spiral $r = a\theta$ ($a = \text{effPitch} / 2\pi$) out to $r_{\max}$.
- **Curve-sampling reduction (`arcStep`-only, `PITCH = 6.2 px` fixed = 100% turns = ~125 turns)**:
  - Increases chord length `arcStep` along the same 125 spiral turns (`1.40 px` Full -> `1.87 px` `75k` -> `2.28 px` `40k` -> `3.84 px` `4k` -> `6.02 px` `2k` -> `9.46 px` `1k` -> `14.86 px` `500`).
  - Because all 125 turns remain present, every horizontal scanline across the `480×480` canvas still intersects ~500 spiral edges during `ctx.fill()`, and below `4k` (`2k`, `1k`, `500`) the inner/mid turns become visibly polygonal (chording).
  - **Note on Old vs New Sub-4k Floor**: Previously, `core.ts` clamped `deriveDraftDetailScale` at `ratio = 0.08` (`6k`) and `mellan.ts` clamped `Math.max(0.32, q.materialDetailScale)`, which caused `4k`, `2k`, `1k`, and `500` to saturate at `156,600`–`137,294` vertices. For the `2k / 1k / 500` owner test (`targetB < 4000`), `mellan.ts` scales `arcStep = (1.4 / 0.365) * (4000 / targetB)^0.65` while leaving `4k` (`4000`, `arcStep = 3.836 px`, `156,600` vertices, hash `8bdcd05c`) and `Full` (`429,038` vertices, hash `bce9edc2`) **100% unchanged**.
- **Spiral Turn-Density (`effPitch = PITCH / turnDensity`, `mellanTurnDensity` = `0.75`, `0.50`, `0.25`)**:
  - Scales the distance between neighbouring turns (`effPitch = 8.27 px` at 75% turns, `12.40 px` at 50% turns, `24.80 px` at 25% turns) while scaling burin half-width proportionally (`w = effPitch * (0.05 + 0.86 * dark) * grow / 2`) as a design intent to preserve optical ink tone (`2w / effPitch`) and keeping smooth curve sampling (`arcStep = 1.62..2.80 px`) so turns stay round.
  - **Non-Linear Work Reduction & Visual Tradeoff**: Total spiral arc length scales as $L \propto 1 / \text{effPitch} = D$, and with `arcStep = 1.4 / \sqrt{D}`, total vertices scale as $D^{3/2}$ while scanline edge intersections per row scale as $D$. **`Pitch 50% Turns` (`154,138` vertices) has nearly the same vertex count as `Draft 4k (arcStep)` (`156,600` vertices), yet renders more than 2× faster in DOM advancing draw (`15.5 ms` vs `33.3 ms` p50 = `64.5 FPS` vs `30.0 FPS` derived draw rate)**. However, because changing turn pitch shifts where spiral turns land relative to the reference `6.20 px` spiral, pixel-level foreground coverage drops to `0.954` (`Pitch 75%`, `9.82 dB` PSNR) and `0.894` (`Pitch 50%`, `8.87 dB` PSNR) and requires **explicit owner visual review**.
- **Measured Full-Mode vs Draft-Mode Limitation in Autonomous Worker Playback (`mellan-worker-playback-summary.json`)**:
  - On the 180-frame (`6.0 s`) sustained peak-spiral (`92%–100%`) autonomous `requestAnimationFrame` + real `AudioContext` (`WebAudioFilmTimeline`) run inside `PlatePlayer` + `WorkerHost`, **`Full` mode required `866.0 ms` initial startup wait, `7.20 s` total wall time for the `6.0 s` clip (`25.0 fps` wall rate), and `79.10 MiB` peak RAM cache** (under the `160 MiB` Full-mode decoded window budget, exceeding the `32.00 MiB` Draft budget by `2.47×`). By contrast, all Draft profiles (`75k`, `40k`, `4k`, `2k`, `1k`, `500`, `Pitch 75%`, `Pitch 50%`, `Pitch 25%`) stayed within **`13.18–26.37 MiB` (`<= 32.00 MiB` Draft budget)** and achieved **`29.9–30.0 fps` wall rate** after `200.8–488.1 ms` startup wait.

## 4. Summary of Unimplemented Follow-Up Hypotheses Identified by the 31-Style Audit

*Note: The items below are **unimplemented, unmeasured engineering hypotheses** derived from source auditing and the single-pass 31-style probe (`all-31-active-frame-audit.json`); any speedup must be verified by implementation and benchmark runs before claiming.* 

1. **Missing `isCalibratingPen()` Guards on 4 Non-`Gfx.pen` Checkpoint Kits (`colouredPencil`, `scratchboard`, `folkTale`, `brokenColour`)**:
   - Because these 4 styles do not call `Gfx.pen`, `calibrateScenePenBudget` currently executes 2 representative frames (`f = 0, mid`) into a throwaway `calEnv.cache` before the first Draft draw (`colouredPencil` `1,022.9 ms -> 1,211.0 ms` cold; `scratchboard` `682.4 ms -> 1,175.8 ms` cold; `folkTale` `320.5 ms -> 398.1 ms` cold; `brokenColour` `231.0 ms -> 292.3 ms` cold). Adding `if (isCalibratingPen()) return;` (as already implemented and verified for `stipple`, `halftone`, `paintedOil`, `embroidery`, `woodcut`, `toyBrick`, `mellan`) is expected to eliminate that throwaway cold calibration pass with zero visual change.
2. **`scratchboard` (`scratchboard.ts`) Cut-Density Striding & Stats Reporting (Unmeasured Hypothesis)**:
   - `scratchboard` currently builds 100% of its `buildCuts()` burin cuts in Draft and does not call `recordOtherPathStats()`. Guarding calibration and striding secondary background/fur cuts in `buildCuts()` with width compensation is a proposed follow-up hypothesis to reduce cold build time while preserving lynx eyes/whiskers/contours.
3. **`colouredPencil` (`colouredPencil.ts` / `colouredPencilCrow.ts`) Interior Grain-Mark Striding (Unmeasured Hypothesis)**:
   - `colouredPencil` generates `546,152` pencil mark vertices during cold build in both Full and Draft. Striding interior fill marks by `materialDetailScale` with mark-width compensation is a proposed follow-up hypothesis to reduce cold build time.
4. **`lighthouse` (`riso.ts`) 2×2 Risograph Drum Grain Sub-Sampling (Unmeasured Hypothesis)**:
   - `lighthouse` spends `117.0 ms` on its 1-frame still redraw in `riso.ts` per-pixel `getImageData`/`putImageData` drum grain/knockout loops (`Gfx.pen` vertices are only `9,287`). Sub-sampling the `riso.ts` grain loop on a `2×2` block in Draft is a proposed follow-up hypothesis to reduce per-frame pixel-loop cost.
5. **`ranunculus` (`core.ts` `Gfx.wash`) Single-Layer Draft Wash (Unmeasured Hypothesis)**:
   - `ranunculus` executes `275` `Gfx.wash()` calls across its `89` petals (`84` ring petals + `5` centre petals, `3` washes each via `g.form()` + `g.wash()`) and `4` stalk/leaf/calyx forms (`8` washes) on its 1-frame plate (`61.8 ms`). Skipping the secondary 0.28-alpha halo-bleed offscreen layer `H` inside `Gfx.wash()` in Draft mode is a proposed follow-up hypothesis to reduce offscreen canvas compositing.
